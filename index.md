## 🗺️ What Is BundesPulse?

BundesPulse is a free, read-only tool that lets you explore Germany like never before. It shows you **111 different indicators** for the entire country, all 16 federal states (Bundesländer), and around 400 districts (Kreise). 

Forget complicated spreadsheets and confusing government websites. BundesPulse turns raw numbers into a colorful, interactive map that anyone can understand. Whether you're a student, a journalist, a curious citizen, or just someone who loves data, this tool is for you.

## 🎯 How to Use BundesPulse

### First Launch

When you first open BundesPulse, you'll see a map of Germany. This is your starting point.

### Navigating the Map

1. **Click** on any Bundesland (federal state) to see its overview
2. **Scroll** to zoom in and out
3. **Click** on a Kreis (district) for more detailed data
4. **Use** the sidebar to switch between different indicators

### Understanding the Colors

Colors on the map range from light (low values) to dark (high values). A legend in the corner explains what each color means for the selected indicator.

### Finding Specific Information

- **Search Bar:** Type a city name or postal code to jump directly to it
- **Indicator Menu:** Browse all 111 indicators by category
- **Comparison Tool:** Select multiple regions to compare them side by side

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### The Application Won't Start
- Make sure you have Windows 10 or later

- Close and reopen BundesPulse
- Ensure your internet connection is stable
